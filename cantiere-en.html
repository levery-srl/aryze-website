The problem

On site, time is money. Searching for information is the problem.

Every hour spent searching for a document, waiting for an answer or reconstructing an activity is a direct cost.

Documents that can't be found

Updated drawings, datasheets, design changes: everyone has a version, no-one has the right one. Work stops while people search.

Support that arrives too late

When an answer is needed, someone calls, waits, sends a photo on WhatsApp. Meanwhile the crew is idle and the site falls behind schedule.

Work not recorded

What was checked, by whom, with what outcome: often undocumented, or written up at the end of the day when details are already lost.

The solution

ARyze connects the physical site to digital information, in real time.

Three core capabilities that eliminate the three main problems of the modern construction site.

01

Instant access to project documentation

Scan the element or search by voice. Drawings, datasheets, specs, in the current version, without opening folders or calling the office.

02

Remote connection with experts

Open a live session with the designer, site manager or supplier. They see exactly what you see. They decide with you, in real time, without travelling.

03

Automatic progress documentation

Photos, notes, completed checks: ARyze structures them into a ready-to-share report. No manual entry at the end of the day.

Who it's for

For those who work on site and those who coordinate them.

ARyze is designed for the professionals who face construction complexity every day.

Main contractors and firms

Team coordination, subcontractor management, progress and non-conformance checks, all documented in real time.

Site managers and inspectors

Tracked site visits, as-built checks, site communications always documented and linked to the project.

Specialist subcontractors

Direct access to work-package specs and remote supplier support, without depending on site coordination.
